Engineering approach

Make the difficult parts
understandable.

01

Start with the boundary.

Define what a system is allowed to do, what it must never assume, and where responsibility changes hands. Then design the interfaces around those rules.

02

Prefer visible state.

Good software makes its decisions inspectable. Clear states, useful traces and honest failure paths beat a polished surface that hides what happened.

03

Ship the smallest real thing.

Keep the architecture deliberate, but let working behaviour decide what deserves to exist. A demonstrable system is more valuable than a speculative feature list.
